Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · 简体中文

Better Day USA Inc. operates iCook Social for Android (package cloud.icook.social.member). This policy covers the member App and its use of the iCook Social service. Our privacy contact is xu@icook.cloud.

The App provides public content browsing and member tools for sharing, training, customer follow-up and viewing settlement records. It uses the existing service at social.icook.cloud and a mobile API hosted on Cloudflare. The separate social-media publishing Hub has its own privacy policy.

Information handled by the App

  • Account and access: email and password when you sign in; the service-issued session; your member and team identifiers, name, email and membership status. These enable sign-in and access to the records your account is allowed to use.
  • Customer records: customer names, phone numbers, WeChat identifiers and notes entered by a member. Contact details may also be submitted to check for existing records before adding a customer. Only enter information you are authorized to provide.
  • Sharing and learning: the content and channel selected, attributed share links, actual publication URLs and times, referral results reported by the service, and course completion records.
  • Searches and technical records: search terms are sent to the service. The source service retains operational request records, including request outcomes and timings; request URLs can contain search terms or contact details used for lookups. IP-based sign-in counters support abuse prevention. A rate-limit window ending does not itself erase its stored counter.
  • Settlement information: the App displays amounts, status and related customer records already held by the service. It does not accept payment cards, process payments or withdrawals, or let members create settlement amounts.

Use and disclosure

Information supports account access, content discovery, attributed sharing, training progress, customer administration, settlement display, support and service security. Team administrators and authorized service personnel may access records within their roles. Infrastructure providers, including Cloudflare, process connection and service data to deliver these functions.

When you use Android sharing, you choose the recipient app or destination. The selected text, link or media is sent to that destination at your direction, and that destination's own privacy practices apply. iCook Social does not automatically publish social-media posts for you. The Android App includes no advertising or cross-app advertising-tracking SDK.

Some course videos are hosted by external media providers. Playing a direct video link connects your device to that provider; other video links open in an external app or browser. The provider may receive your IP address and media request information, and its privacy practices apply. The member session used with our mobile API is not attached to external video requests.

Connection and device storage

App-to-service requests use HTTPS. Sign-in credentials go directly to the existing login service. The Android App does not save your password. It stores the validated session encrypted with a key protected by Android Keystore, and keeps the language preference on the device. App data backup is disabled.

Media used for sharing is held in a bounded temporary cache and may be removed by cache maintenance or sign-out. Media you explicitly save goes to the device's Downloads/iCook Social folder and remains there until you manage it. Signing out removes the App's local session and temporary media; it does not revoke sessions on other devices or erase server records.

Device permissions

The App does not request access to your address book, location, camera, microphone or broad photo library. It uses Android's Downloads collection to save selected files and grants a chosen sharing destination temporary access to the specific shared file. Public browsing does not require a member login; private functions require a valid member account.

Retention and your choices

Service records are retained while needed for the member service, customer attribution, settlement administration, security and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Account deactivation is different from deletion of the related records. Where a retention obligation applies, data may remain for that purpose.

You may contact xu@icook.cloud to request access, correction, account closure or deletion of eligible personal data. We verify identity and authority before acting, including requests involving another person's customer information. Requests are handled manually; we explain any records that must be retained and why. See data request instructions.

The App uses existing team accounts and has no account-registration feature. Its member tools are intended for adult team members. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact our privacy address.

This information website

These public information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. We have added no analytics, advertising scripts or data-entry forms to these pages.

Policy updates

Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Contact Better Day USA Inc. at xu@icook.cloud for questions about this policy.

简体中文

Android App 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日。Better Day USA Inc. 运营 iCook Social Android 成员 App，包名为 cloud.icook.social.member。隐私联系邮箱：xu@icook.cloud。本政策适用于成员 App 及其对既有 iCook Social 服务的使用；独立的社媒发布 Hub 使用自己的隐私政策。

我们处理的信息

登录时使用邮箱、密码及服务签发的会话；服务返回成员姓名、邮箱、成员与团队标识及权限状态。成员录入的客户姓名、电话、微信标识和备注会发送至既有服务，查重时也会使用联系方式。分享功能处理所选内容、渠道、专属链接、实际发布链接和时间，以及服务记录的转化统计；学习功能记录课程完成状态。请仅录入你有权提供的信息。

搜索词会发送至服务。源站保留请求结果、处理耗时等运行记录；请求网址可能包含搜索词或客户查重使用的联系方式。源站还保留用于防滥用的 IP 登录限流记录，限流时间窗结束不代表该记录已删除。App 展示既有结算金额、状态和关联客户信息，不处理支付、银行卡、提现，也不由成员创建结算金额。

用途与数据流向

数据用于账号访问、内容发现、分享归属、课程进度、客户管理、结算展示、支持和安全。团队管理员及获授权的服务人员在其权限范围内访问相关记录。Cloudflare 等基础设施服务商为提供服务处理连接及运行数据。你使用系统分享时，由你选择目标应用；所选内容传递给该目标，其隐私规则适用于后续处理。App 不自动发布社交媒体帖子，不包含广告或跨应用广告追踪 SDK。

部分课程视频由外部媒体服务托管。播放视频直链会让设备直接连接对应提供方；其他视频链接通过外部应用或浏览器打开。提供方可收到 IP 地址和媒体请求信息，其隐私规则适用。发送到外部视频地址的请求不附带用于我们移动 API 的成员会话。

设备上的信息与权限

App 与服务之间使用 HTTPS。密码直接发送到既有登录服务，Android App 不保存密码。已验证的会话通过 Android Keystore 保护的密钥加密保存，语言偏好保存在本机，应用数据备份已关闭。分享素材使用有容量限制的临时缓存，可由缓存维护或退出登录清理；你主动保存至 Downloads/iCook Social 的文件会保留，供你自行管理。退出登录移除本机会话及临时素材，不撤销其他设备的会话或删除服务端记录。

App 不申请通讯录、定位、相机、麦克风或整库照片读取权限；保存素材使用 Android 下载目录，系统分享仅临时授权目标应用读取所选文件。公开内容可游客浏览，私有功能要求有效成员账号。

保留、访问与删除

服务记录在提供成员服务、客户归属、结算管理、安全及适用记录保存义务所需期间保留。账号停用不等于关联记录已删除。你可通过上述邮箱申请访问、更正、账号关闭或删除符合条件的数据。处理前会核验身份及操作权限；涉及他人的客户信息还需核验处理授权。请求由人工处理，必须保留的记录及原因会在回复中说明。详见数据请求说明。

App 使用团队提供的既有账号，没有账号注册功能，成员工具面向成年团队成员。如认为儿童提供了个人信息，请联系隐私邮箱。本说明网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者 IP；这些页面没有额外加入统计、广告脚本或数据填写表单。政策变更会更新本页面和生效日期。
