Data deletion requests

Better Day USA Inc. handles requests concerning the iCook Social Android member App. You can request account closure, deletion of eligible personal data, or correction of information.

  1. Email xu@icook.cloud with the subject iCook Social Android data request.
  2. Include your member account email and team name if known. State whether you want to close the account, delete specific records, or correct information.
  3. We may ask for information to verify your identity and authority over the records. Keep passwords and session tokens out of the request.

Requests are processed manually. Eligible account, profile and related member records are reviewed for deletion. Customer, attribution or settlement records can involve other people or recordkeeping obligations; any necessary retention and its reason will be explained in the response. Deactivation alone does not erase all service records.

Signing out clears the App's local session and temporary media. It does not delete your server account. Files you saved in Downloads/iCook Social remain on your device and can be managed with Android's file manager.

The App supports existing team accounts and does not offer account registration or automated account deletion.

数据删除与账号关闭

请向 xu@icook.cloud 发送请求，主题填写“iCook Social Android data request”。提供成员账号邮箱、已知的团队名称，以及需要关闭账号、删除哪些记录或更正哪些信息。请勿发送密码或会话令牌。

Better Day USA Inc. 会人工核验身份及处理权限，并审查符合删除条件的账号、资料和成员记录。涉及其他人的客户信息、归属或结算记录，可能需要相应授权或依法保留；回复会说明保留内容及原因。账号停用不等于所有记录已经删除。

App 退出登录只清除本机会话和临时素材，不删除服务端账号。已保存至 Downloads/iCook Social 的文件可通过 Android 文件管理器自行管理。App 使用既有团队账号，不提供注册或自动账号注销。
