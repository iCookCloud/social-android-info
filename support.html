Android App support

How can we help?

Support for iCook Social for Android, operated by Better Day USA Inc.

Member access

Private features require an existing USER member account supplied by your team administrator. Administrator and super-administrator accounts use the website and cannot sign in to this member App. Contact your team administrator for membership or activation questions.

For password recovery, use the existing service's password recovery page.

Sharing and downloads

Use the Android share sheet to choose where to send selected content. After publishing in the destination app, return to record the actual post URL and publication time. Opening the share sheet alone does not publish a post. Saved files appear in Downloads/iCook Social.

Customers and settlements

Ask your team administrator about customer ownership, duplicate records or settlement amounts. The App shows the service's existing records and does not process payments or withdrawals.

Contact support

Email xu@icook.cloud. Include your App version, Android version, affected screen and the problem you encountered. Omit passwords, session tokens and other people's customer details.
